# 📌 Executive Summary

Human Resources is one of the most strategic functions within any organization. Every hiring decision, promotion, salary adjustment, performance evaluation, and employee exit contributes valuable data that can shape business success. However, when this information is incomplete, inconsistent, or scattered across spreadsheets, organizations struggle to gain meaningful insights that support informed decision-making.

This project presents an **end-to-end Human Resources Analytics solution** developed for **Apex Bank Plc**, a fictional Nigerian commercial bank, using **Microsoft Power BI**. The objective was to transform a raw employee dataset containing over **2,000 employee records** into a reliable, interactive dashboard capable of supporting executive-level HR decision-making.

The project began with extensive data preparation in **Power Query**, where inconsistencies such as invalid salary values, mixed date formats, inconsistent department names, formatting issues, and logical conflicts were identified and resolved. A clean analytical model was then built, followed by the development of business-focused DAX measures and an interactive dashboard that provides a comprehensive view of workforce performance.

Rather than serving as a collection of charts, this dashboard functions as a **decision-support system**, enabling HR managers and executives to monitor workforce distribution, salary trends, employee performance, attrition, tenure, leave utilization, and overall data quality from a single reporting environment.

The final solution demonstrates the complete Business Intelligence lifecycle—from understanding business requirements and improving data quality to delivering actionable insights that support workforce planning, employee retention, compensation analysis, and strategic HR management.

# 💼 Business Challenge

Human Resource departments manage large volumes of employee information across multiple business functions, including recruitment, employee demographics, compensation, performance evaluations, leave management, and workforce planning. As organizations grow, maintaining accurate and consistent HR data becomes increasingly challenging.

For Apex Bank Plc, the HR dataset contained several data quality issues that limited its ability to support reliable reporting and strategic decision-making. These challenges included inconsistent department names, invalid salary values, inconsistent text formatting, mixed date formats, incomplete contact information, and logical inconsistencies between employee status and exit records.

Without addressing these issues, key workforce metrics such as employee headcount, average salary, attrition rate, tenure, and performance analysis could produce misleading results. Inaccurate reporting can lead to poor workforce planning, ineffective compensation strategies, increased employee turnover, and inefficient resource allocation.

The challenge was therefore not simply to build a dashboard, but to develop a complete Business Intelligence solution that transforms unreliable HR data into trusted information capable of supporting executive decision-making.

This project addresses that challenge by combining data cleaning, transformation, analytical modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visualizations to provide HR leadership with a reliable view of organizational performance and workforce dynamics.

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objective of this project was to develop an interactive Human Resources Analytics Dashboard capable of supporting strategic workforce management through data-driven insights.

### Business Objectives

- Determine the total number of active employees after data cleaning.
- Understand workforce distribution across departments and grade levels.
- Evaluate gender representation across the organization.
- Analyze salary distribution across employee grades.
- Monitor employee performance across departments.
- Measure employee tenure and workforce retention.
- Evaluate employee attrition trends.
- Analyze annual leave utilization.
- Assess the quality of HR data before business reporting.
- Support evidence-based HR decision-making.

### Technical Objectives

- Clean and transform raw HR data using Power Query.
- Standardize inconsistent records and improve data quality.
- Build a reliable analytical model in Power BI.
- Develop DAX measures for key HR metrics.
- Design an executive-level interactive dashboard.
- Present complex HR information through clear and intuitive visualizations.
- Demonstrate best practices in Business Intelligence, Data Analytics, and Data Storytelling.
